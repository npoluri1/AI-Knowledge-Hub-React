    Oman, Bahrain, Jordan), Africa (Mauritius, Rwanda, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe),

    40+ jurisdictions: Many African states (Botswana, Tanzania, Ethiopia, Angola, Mozambique,
    Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire), Caribbean (Jamaica, Bahamas, Barbados), Pacific Islands,
    Central Asia (Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan)

  Level 4 (None/Minimal) — No comprehensive privacy law, limited enforcement:
    30+ jurisdictions: Small island states, conflict zones, least developed countries
    (Afghanistan, North Korea, Yemen, Syria, Somalia, South Sudan, Eritrea, etc.)

For a universal global application, minimum compliance baseline across all 198 jurisdictions:
  - Adhere to GDPR (EU/EEA) as highest common denominator — many laws are GDPR-aligned
  - Implement NIST CSF 2.0 / ISO 27001 for security baseline
  - Support ISO 42001 / EU AI Act for AI features
  - Map data residency requirements per country (35+ countries require data localization)
  - Implement cross-border transfer mechanisms: SCCs (EU, UK), DPF (US), CBPR (APEC),
    Adequacy decisions, SCC equivalents (China, India, Brazil, Vietnam, etc.)
  - Breach notification: track 72-hr (GDPR, Malaysia, Korea) / 48-hr (Peru) / "as soon as
    practicable" (varies) requirements
  - DPO appointment: mandatory in EU/EEA, UK, Korea, Malaysia (2024), Vietnam, Japan, etc.
  - Consent management: varying standards (opt-in vs opt-out, explicit vs implied)
  - AI regulation: track EU AI Act, China GenAI, Brazil Bill 2338, Canada AIDA, US EO/state,
    Japan AI Act, Korea AI Basic Act, Singapore AI Verify, India AI strategy
